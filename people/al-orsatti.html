Al Orsatti

Brother of Ernie Orsatti

Al worked alongside his brothers Frank, Vic and Ernie in the Orsatti Agency. The family business represented performers and creative talent across film and radio; Al’s own clients included actress Peggy O’Neill.

He was the father of Ken, born Alfred Kendall Orsatti, who later became the Screen Actors Guild’s national executive director. Al died of a heart attack by 1949, aged 48; the agency’s subsequent leadership passed to Vic after both Al and Frank had died.
